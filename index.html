How much do I pay to start?

A 50% deposit starts production after the design and quote are agreed. The balance is due before collection or delivery.

How long does production take?

Approximately 3 business days after the deposit and design details are confirmed. We’ll confirm your estimated ready date.

Can you deliver outside Ruaka?

Delivery may be arranged at an extra cost, depending on your location. We’ll confirm the fee before you approve the order.
