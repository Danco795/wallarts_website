GOOD TO KNOW

Before you order.

How much do I pay to get started?

A 50% deposit is required to start your order after the design and quote are agreed. The remaining balance is paid before collection or delivery.

How long does an order take?

Allow approximately 3 business days after your deposit and design approval. We’ll confirm the estimated ready date with you; some portrait preferences or designs may need more time.

Can you deliver my order?

Yes. Delivery costs extra and depend on your location, especially for areas outside nearby Nairobi environs. We’ll confirm the delivery fee before you approve the order.

When should I come to collect?

We’ll notify you when your artwork is ready. Collection is through the Ruaka front desk; please wait for our message and confirm the collection details before travelling.
