GOOD TO KNOW

Before you order.

How much do I pay upfront?

We usually begin with a 50% deposit once the size, style and price are agreed. The remaining balance is due before pickup or delivery.

Can I get custom sizes?

Yes. We can help you decide based on the room, the image and the mood you want. Different sizes can be arranged for a more personalised fit.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes. Delivery costs extra and is confirmed before you approve the order, especially for areas outside nearby Nairobi environs.

What happens if I am not in Nairobi?

We can arrange delivery at an extra cost, or you can wait for a ready-for-pickup confirmation depending on your location and preference.
